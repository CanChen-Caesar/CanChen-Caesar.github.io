While the Macroscopic Fundamental Diagram (MFD) provides a framework for large-scale ground traffic modeling, conventional formulations often fail to capture the velocity losses incurred during three-dimensional, multi-directional flight caused by collision avoidance maneuvers. To address this, we propose an enhanced MFD formulation that accounts for speed heterogeneity and path elongation by incorporating the statistical moments of projected speed, defined as the component of drone velocity along the shortest origin--destination direction. This model is extended to accommodate heterogeneous drone groups with different priorities, specifically modeling a system where prioritized drones operate independently of standard traffic to maintain efficiency. Extensive simulations demonstrate that the proposed LAAT MFD model improves estimation accuracy across various operational scales, enabling robust real-time estimation and departure management. This framework provides a computationally scalable approach for optimizing throughput and efficiency in heterogeneous urban low-altitude airspace.
